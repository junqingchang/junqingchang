## About

I train models, then build the thing that lets someone else use them.

My PhD was on **compositionality** — teaching models to recognise things by their parts. At AMD I work the applied end of the same idea: solving computer-vision problems in production, and building them into applications other teams use.

- 🔬 **PhD, Artificial Intelligence** — Nanyang Technological University, 2020–2025 · *Learning Compositionality for Image Classification and Segmentation*
- 🏭 **Senior Product Development Engineer, Data Science** — AMD Singapore, Manufacturing Strategy
- 🎓 **BEng, Information Systems Technology and Design** (AI specialisation) — SUTD, Magna Cum Laude
- 🇸🇬 Based in Singapore · happy to talk about applied research, computer vision, and generative-model work
